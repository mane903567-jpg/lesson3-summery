4. Important Exam Points
The SI unit of length is the meter (m).
The suitable instrument for measuring long lengths is the measuring tape.
The suitable instrument for measuring ordinary lengths is the ruler.
The suitable instrument for measuring small lengths is the Vernier calipers.
Vernier calipers have a higher accuracy than a ruler and measuring tape.
Remember: Smaller accuracy values mean more precise measurements.













1. Vernier Caliper (القدمة ذات الورنية)
Definition:
A Vernier caliper is an instrument used to measure lengths accurately.
Structure:
1 \ It consists of two jaws attached to:
Fixed scale.
Sliding (Vernier) scale.
Divisions:
One division on the fixed scale = 1 mm.
One division on the Vernier scale = 0.99 mm.
The difference between them = 0.01 mm.
Procedure:
Place the object between the two jaws and gently press.
Read the fixed scale just before the zero mark of the Vernier scale.
Find the Vernier division that aligns with a mark on the fixed scale.
Multiply the aligned division number by 0.1 mm and add it to the main scale reading.

2. Divisions (التدريجات)
The micrometer measures small lengths accurately.
The screw advances a known distance when the drum rotates once.
The screw usually advances by 0.5 mm or 1 mm per rotation.
Each division may represent 0.01 mm.
3. Zero Error (خطأ الصفر)
Before measuring, close the jaws and check that the micrometer reads zero.
No zero error: No correction is needed.
Positive zero error (+): Subtract the error from the measurement.
Negative zero error (−): Add the error to the measurement.







4. Measuring Procedure (خطوات القياس)
Check the zero error.
Place the object between the jaws carefully.
Record the fixed scale reading.
Record the movable scale reading.
Calculate the movable scale reading:
Movable reading = Number of divisions × 0.01 mm
6. Calculate the final measurement:
Final reading = Fixed scale reading + Movable scale reading
7. Correct the final reading for any zero error.
