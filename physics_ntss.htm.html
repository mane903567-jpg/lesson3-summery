Lesson 4

Lesson 4: Mass and Weight measurements

Meaning of Mass (m)

In our daily life:

1) Mass is the quantity of matter in a physical body.

In physics:

2) Mass is the Resistance of object to change its Kinematic state.

Meaning of Weight

In our daily life:

1) Weight is the measure of the heaviness of an object.

In physics:

2) Weight is the force of gravity acting on the object OR is the force with which a body is pulled towards the earth.

• Its direction is towards the Earth's center.

The Equation

Fg = m × g

Symbols Meanings:

Fg : Weight (Force of gravity)

m : Mass of the object

g : Acceleration due to gravity or acceleration of free fall

Key Concepts

1) Mass and weight are related: Bodies having large mass also have large weight. A large stone is hard to throw because of its large mass, and hard to lift off the ground because of its large weight hence the magnitude (Fg) of a body's weight is directly proportional to its mass (m).

2) In fact, the value of g varies somewhat from point to point on the earth's surface because the earth is not perfectly spherical and because of effects due to its rotation and orbital motion. Also the weight of a body varies from one location to another; the mass does not.

Measuring unit of Mass and Weight

• Mass is measured in Kilogram (kg).

• Weight is a vector quantity measured in Newton (N).
